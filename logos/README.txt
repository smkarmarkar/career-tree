=================================
Drop official logo files here (from each company press or brand kit).
Name each file exactly as below. .svg is best, .png or .jpg also work. Square or wide logos on a transparent or white background look best.
Any company without a file shows a lettered badge instead.
To use a different path, add  logo: "logos/whatever.png"  to that company in the data file.
